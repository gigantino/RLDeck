RLDeck

A lightweight custom map loader for Rocket League.

Download

Grab the zip from the releases page, unpack it anywhere, and run RLDeck.exe.

What it does

Explore
Browse the catalog from lethamyr.com without leaving the app. Pick a map and it downloads into your library.
Library
Your collection. Maps you download from Explore land here, and Import brings in ones you already have, whether that's a zip, a folder, or a .upk.
Load
Replace Underpass with your preferred map by clicking on Load, to be able to host with Rocket Plugin or play in a private match.

Source

Open source on GitHub. Bugs and suggestions go in the issue tracker.
